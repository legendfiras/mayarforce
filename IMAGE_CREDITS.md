# Images

The hero banner and circular logo are the supplied Mayar Force artwork, stored locally:

- `public/images/mayar/hero-banner.jpg` — completed banner (logo on the left, owner on the right)
- `public/images/mayar/logo.png` — circular badge extracted from the original screenshot, with transparency outside the circle
- `public/images/mayar/favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`, `favicon-192.png`, `favicon-512.png`
- `public/images/mayar/apple-touch-icon.png`

Product photographs in `public/images/products/` were downloaded from the manufacturer pages listed in `src/content/product-image-manifest.ts`. They are for this local demo. Reuse permission has not been verified, and the files are not cleared for publication. Products that never received a photo were removed from the catalog. The shop logo is not used as a product image.
